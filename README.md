## 📖 What It Does & The Problem It Solves

**Hassan Butt's Portfolio** is a modern, interactive personal portfolio website that showcases my work, skills, and experience as a Software Engineering student at COMSATS University Sahiwal. It serves as a professional online presence to attract clients, employers, and collaborators.

### The Problem
Students and freelancers need a professional online presence to showcase their work, skills, and experience to potential clients and employers. Without a well-designed portfolio, it's difficult to:
- Build credibility and trust
- Attract freelance clients
- Stand out to employers
- Network with industry professionals

### The Solution
This portfolio provides:
- A professional showcase of my projects and skills
- Interactive 3D effects for an engaging experience
- AI-powered career insights to demonstrate technical expertise
- Dark/Light mode for user comfort
- Contact form for potential clients to reach out

### Who It's For
- 🎓 **Potential Employers** - See my skills and projects
- 💼 **Clients** - Hire me for freelance work
- 🤝 **Network** - Connect with me professionally
- 👨‍💻 **Tech Enthusiasts** - Explore my technical stack
